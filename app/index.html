      Roadmap to the platinum

      About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). Four stages, in order. The story comes first — every story trophy pops as you play — then the robberies and side strands, then the long activities and collectibles sweep, and finally the 100% checklist, which only opens after the credits.

        Before you start

        • No online trophies. All 55 are single-player; the game launches without a new GTA Online mode.
        • Cheats disable trophies — and you cannot save once one is used. Cheats only unlock after the story.
        • No mission replay, no New Game+. Keep manual saves at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
        • One possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude (see the banner on the Tracker). Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable.
